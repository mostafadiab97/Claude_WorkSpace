# Requirements Management Tools — Market List

Compiled 31 Oct 2026 for the ReqMan branding work (naming and competitor screening).

About 190 tools, grouped by kind. No single directory lists them all: G2 alone tracks 79 products in its
requirements-management category, and new AI and Jira add-ons appear every month. Treat this as the broadest
list a web search could assemble, not as a complete census.

**How to read it**
- **Source** links to the page where the tool was found. "Memory" means I know the product but did not confirm it on a page during this search. Check those before relying on them.
- Vendors are given where the source named them, or where they are well known.
- Notes are only enough to place each tool. They are not reviews.

## What this means for naming (ReqMan)

- **"Req-" names are already common:** ReqView, reqSuite, ReqTest, Reqtify, Reqi, reqT, ReqPipeline, RequirementOne, RequirementsHub, Requisor. This confirms the decision to avoid "Req".
- **"Trace-" names are just as common:** TraceCloud, Trace.Space, TraceLynx, Traceable, TraceabilityLite, Vivid Trace, OpenFastTrace, flashtrace.
- **DOORS puns are taken:** Doorstop, Doorhole.
- **None of the Phase 3 shortlist names (Stet, Seshat, Plumb, Stele, Clew, Heron, Rubric) appears as a requirements tool in this list.** That is a good sign, but it is not trademark clearance.
